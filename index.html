Kenmore / Capital Equipment

Capital equipment,
engineered to your circuit.

Crushers, mills, screens and other capital equipment — designed by Kenmore engineers and supplied as individual machines or as part of a project, where we deliver fully integrated circuits. Every machine is sized to your ore, your throughput and your duty cycle, not pulled off a generic catalogue.

01 / Crushers

Jaw Crushers

A modular, weld-free frame structure built for the toughest African ores — abrasive, hard and high-moisture. Cast steel components and premium spherical roller bearings deliver high fatigue resistance and a long, reliable service life across primary and secondary duty.

Feed opening
from 800×510 mm to 2,000×1,500 mm
Power
from 75 kW to 400 kW
Max. capacity
from 65 t/h to 1,460 t/h
Application
Primary, secondary

01 / Crushers

Cone Crushers

Secondary and tertiary crushers for hard and medium-hard ores. Available in standard and short-head configurations across hydraulic and single-cylinder hydraulic ranges — with automatic overload protection, CSS adjustment and consistent product gradation across the full capacity range.

Feed size
from 35 mm to 460 mm
Power
from 22 kW to 750 kW
Max. capacity

Application
Secondary, tertiary

01 / Crushers

Gyratory Crushers

Primary crushers engineered for high-tonnage operations handling large feed sizes. Continuous compressive crushing within a circular chamber gives high throughput and low energy per tonne — well suited to large-scale production plants and high-capacity quarry operations.

Feed size
from 1,066 mm to 1,525 mm
Power
from 450 kW to 1,500 kW
Max. capacity
from 5,250 t/h to 17,000 t/h
Application
Primary crushing

Ball, SAG, AG & Rod Mills

Shell diameter
from 1,200 mm to 6,000 mm
Shell length
from 2,400 mm to 9,500 mm
Power

Configurations
